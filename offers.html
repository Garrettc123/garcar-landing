NO SALES GIMMICKS

Questions worth answering before you buy.

Can I see something useful without paying?

Yes. The free on-device CSV check shows up to ten prioritized rows with their reasons. It does not upload your list to Garcar or perform outbound contact.

Do you claim every flagged record is a missed call?

No. An absent field is not proof a callback was missed. We distinguish documented response gaps from missing or unverified evidence.

Do I need Follow Up Boss or another CRM?

Not for an eligible review. A suitable export or tracker can be enough. An installation, if requested, depends on your actual system and an agreed scope.

What if my export isn't usable?

We first confirm the required fields and scope. If the data cannot support the proposed deliverable, we discuss options and the applicable service terms before proceeding.

Can you guarantee more closed deals?

No. We provide a concrete analysis and suggested next actions; outcomes depend on lead quality, team action, customer consent and other factors outside Garcar's control.

How do I provide the CSV securely?

Do not attach customer lists to a first inquiry email. We confirm minimum necessary fields and a suitable private transfer method after discussing the project.
