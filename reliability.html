System design

The operating system behind the offer.

The product promise should stay narrower than the long-term platform vision. Today, the real system is a service-and-controls layer around risky workflows; over time, that can mature into broader reliability infrastructure.

01

Observe

Capture each run, tool call, state change, and external write with enough context to reconstruct incidents accurately.

02

Protect

Define policy checks before sensitive actions such as CRM writes, payment operations, record deletion, or outbound communication.

03

Recover

Design approval queues, replay, incident logs, and rollback paths so failures can be contained instead of spreading silently.

04

Verify

Test workflow changes against known failure cases before rollout and keep regression checks tied to business actions, not just text quality.

Truthful positioning rule: do not claim autonomous repair, cross-customer learning, guaranteed prevention, or enterprise audit-grade controls unless those controls are live, documented, and contractually supported.
