Buyer questions

Clear scope. No invented guarantees.

What lead fields do I need?

A lead-created timestamp with timezone is essential for response timing. A first-contact field, a next-follow-up field, owner and status improve coverage. Missing fields are explicitly labeled, not treated as proof of a missed call.

Do you contact my customers?

No. This review analyzes a buyer-owned export. It does not call, text, email, or change CRM records. Any later outbound automation requires a separately approved scope and authorization.

Do I need to replace my CRM?

No. Start with a compatible exported CSV. A future implementation is a separate project with its own scope and access controls.

Is this a guaranteed increase in revenue?

No. We deliver a record-based prioritization and findings report. Source quality and operational response determine what can be learned and acted upon.
