Interview standards

  • Use voluntary, permission-based conversations.
  • Do not record sensitive medical, account, or trauma details.
  • Record objections as well as positive feedback.
  • Five qualified conversations are directional evidence, not market proof.
  • Follow-up permission is not blanket marketing consent.
