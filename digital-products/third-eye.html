The question behind the symbol

The pineal gland is a small endocrine structure best known for producing melatonin, a hormone involved in circadian timing. The third eye, meanwhile, is a spiritual symbol associated in different traditions with insight and discernment. Those are related topics of curiosity, not proof that the gland is a supernatural receiver.
