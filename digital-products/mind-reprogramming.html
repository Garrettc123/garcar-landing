Choose a behavior you can observe

Replace “be more disciplined” with one concrete action, such as ten minutes of skill practice after breakfast. Make the first version small enough to repeat on a difficult day.

Connect a cue to an action

Write an implementation intention: “After [specific cue], I will [small behavior].” A clear cue makes the start less dependent on remembering or feeling motivated.

Make the useful action easier

Prepare materials, remove one distraction, and define what “done” means before starting. Use a short focus block and write the next step before stopping.
