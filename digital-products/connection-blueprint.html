Listen before responding

Reflect the core message you heard and ask whether you understood correctly. Listening does not require agreement; it creates a better basis for an honest response.

Make a clear request

Try: “When [specific event] happened, I felt [emotion], because [need or value]. I would like [specific request].” A request is not a demand, and the other person remains free to respond.

Set boundaries you control

A boundary describes what you will do to protect your time, safety, or values. It is not a tool for controlling another person. Make it specific, realistic, and within your control.
