Start with a measurable outcome

“I want more money” is understandable, but difficult to act on. Define one outcome you can measure: a bill brought current, a starter cash buffer, a debt balance reduced, or a specific paid service sold.

See the numbers clearly

Map take-home income, essential expenses, minimum debt payments, and due dates using actual records. The first objective is visibility. If essentials exceed income, focus on stabilization and contact relevant providers early.

Build an income habit

Choose a real problem you can solve for a defined customer. Have conversations before spending on ads or software. Track qualified conversations, proposals, and paid work separately. Interest is not revenue; an invoice is not collected cash.
