# AI Revenue Ops Mini-Course

## Lesson 1 — Find the leak
Map one lead-to-cash workflow and locate the first measurable failure.

## Lesson 2 — Build the evidence layer
Capture timestamps, source, owner, status and outcome. Avoid unsupported assumptions.

## Lesson 3 — Automate the clock
Define response, follow-up and escalation rules.

## Lesson 4 — Connect the money
Connect completed work to payment events and verify the payment before counting revenue.

## Lesson 5 — Govern the system
Use idempotency, audit logs, approval gates and failure handling.

## Lesson 6 — Improve
Review failures weekly and turn recurring failures into prevention rules.

### Recording format
Each lesson: 6–10 minutes, one screen demonstration, one worksheet, one implementation task.
