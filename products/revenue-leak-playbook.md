## The 30-minute audit
- List every lead source.
- Record where each lead lands.
- Identify who owns the first response.
- Measure the time from lead arrival to first human/system action.
- Count leads with no second follow-up.
- Identify duplicate or orphaned leads.
- Write one explicit escalation rule.
- Automate only the bottleneck you can measure.

## Core rule
Do not automate a mystery. Measure the failure, define the desired state, then automate the transition.
