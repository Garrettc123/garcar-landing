## Starter prompts

### 1. Lead triage
Classify this new lead by urgency, service fit, location, requested work, and missing information. Return a short action queue and identify anything requiring a human decision.

### 2. Callback clock
Given lead timestamp, current time, business hours, and prior attempts, determine the next allowed follow-up action. Never invent contact attempts.

### 3. Revenue leak review
Review this week's lead records and identify unanswered, delayed, duplicated, or ownerless opportunities. Cite the evidence for every finding.

### 4. Handoff
Create a concise handoff containing customer need, urgency, source, next action, owner, and evidence. Do not add facts not present in the record.

### 5. Daily owner brief
Summarize open revenue opportunities, stalled follow-ups, payments received, failures, and decisions requiring the owner today.

## SOP template
Trigger → Evidence → Decision → Action → Verification → Escalation → Memory.
