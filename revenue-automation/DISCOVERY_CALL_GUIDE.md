# Revenue Automation — Discovery Call Guide

## Purpose
Determine whether an inbound-lead workflow has a measurable problem and whether a small, bounded audit is appropriate. This is an interview guide, not a cold-outreach campaign.

## Opening
- Confirm the person's role and responsibility for lead response.
- Explain the purpose, time needed, and whether notes will be taken.
- Confirm permission to retain non-sensitive business-process notes.
- Do not record audio or quote the person publicly without separate permission.

## Workflow questions
1. Which channels create new opportunities: phone, web form, referral, marketplace, or other?
2. What happens from the moment a lead arrives until a staff member owns it?
3. Who handles business-hours leads? Who handles after-hours leads?
4. What happens when the assigned person is unavailable?
5. Where is the source of truth for lead status and next action?
6. How are duplicate records detected?
7. How do you know whether a lead was contacted?
8. What is your current first-response target, and how do you measure it?
9. What percentage of leads have no recorded next step?
10. What is the smallest workflow change that would make the biggest operational difference?

## Evidence questions
- Can the business provide an authorized, minimized sample of event records?
- What fields are included and which can be removed?
- What time period is representative?
- How are timestamps, time zones, and statuses defined?
- Can the process be evaluated using synthetic data first?
- Who has authority to approve access and scope?

Do not request passwords, payment data, full customer records, health information, or other unnecessary sensitive data.

## Commercial qualification
- Is there an accountable buyer?
- Is the problem important enough to prioritize?
- Can the problem be measured from available evidence?
- Is one workflow feasible without a broad system migration?
- Is there a named process owner?
- Is there a plausible budget path for a fixed-scope review?
- What would make the business reject the proposed scope?

## Fit decision
**Good fit:** one repeated workflow problem, an accountable buyer, evidence available with permission, and a measurable acceptance criterion.

**Not ready:** unclear ownership, no permission to inspect evidence, no baseline, request for broad autonomous operations, or expectation of guaranteed revenue.

## Close
Summarize the observed need and unknowns. Confirm only a mutually agreed next step. Do not create a proposal, send follow-up, or access any customer data without permission.
