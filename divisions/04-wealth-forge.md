# Division 04 — Garcar Wealth Forge

## Mission
Provide practical financial education and business cash-flow intelligence for founders and operators. The division does not custody funds, execute investments, provide individualized securities recommendations, or promise wealth outcomes.

Pricing must be tested through interviews and sample reviews.

## Sales system
Target early-stage founders and owner-operated businesses. Validate the exact job: runway, collections forecasting, bill prioritization, revenue quality, or reporting discipline. Demonstrate with fabricated sample data. Do not request bank credentials or sensitive records for free validation. Any future real-data implementation requires documented consent, access boundaries, retention, and deletion rules.

## Operating model
Inputs are user-entered or owner-authorized data. Calculations are deterministic and documented. Outputs show cash position, expected inflows/outflows, runway scenarios, variance explanations, freshness, and missing coverage. User confirms inputs and decisions; the product does not move money or initiate trades.

## Claims and compliance
Educational material is not personalized financial, tax, legal, or investment advice. Do not imply guaranteed income, investment performance, debt relief, or financial security. Refer individualized regulated questions to qualified professionals.

## Metrics
Qualified interviews; template completion; forecast variance; data completeness; repeat usage; willingness to pay; support burden; verified collections.

## Release gates
Calculation review; assumptions and limitations visible; synthetic sample data; privacy/financial review; customer validation.

## Status
The Abundance Blueprint is an existing introductory educational manuscript in Digital Products. Wealth Forge as a dedicated software/product division remains planned; no financial dashboard or live financial integration is verified.
