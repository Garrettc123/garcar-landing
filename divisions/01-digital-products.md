## Product guardrails
- Distinguish science from spirituality. No unsupported pineal activation, supernatural ability, disease cure, or manifestation claims.
- Wealth education must not promise income, investment returns, debt elimination, or guaranteed outcomes.
- Performance content must not claim to diagnose or treat a condition.
- Relationship content must not guarantee reconciliation or replace professional/safety support.
- Proposed prices are hypotheses, not active prices or accepted offers.

## Sales system
1. Use existing preview pages and samples as validation assets.
2. Collect feedback only from people who initiate contact or explicitly opt in.
3. Record product ID, date, source, stated need, feedback, and follow-up permission.
4. Validate five qualified conversations per market before producing the remaining planned titles.
5. Count a sale only after processor confirmation and reconciliation.

## Delivery architecture
The existing delivery plan defines future server-side checkout, fixed catalog IDs, webhook signature verification, event idempotency, paid-only entitlement, expiring downloads, refund/dispute handling, consent ledger, and audit events. Keep paid assets outside the public static repository. Do not activate checkout in this zero-cost phase.

## Metrics
Qualified conversations by market; sample-to-interview rate; stated willingness to pay; objections; product quality defects; future refund rate; verified net collections.

## Release gates
Manuscript and copy proofread; rights/source review; price and refund terms approved; privacy/support details approved; test-mode checkout and fulfillment verified after explicit authorization; production release approved.
