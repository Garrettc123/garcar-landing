# Division 06 — Garcar Industry Solutions

## Mission
Turn reusable capabilities into vertical-specific solutions with clear workflows, data boundaries, and measurable outcomes.

## Initial vertical catalog
| Offer | Vertical | Product concept | Initial buyer |
|---|---|---|---|
| IS-001 | Construction & home services | Lead intake, estimate follow-up, job-status workflow | Contractor owner / office manager |
| IS-002 | Logistics | Dispatch exception desk and document intake | Dispatcher / operations manager |
| IS-003 | Manufacturing | Shift handoff, maintenance intake, operational reporting | Plant / operations manager |

These are concepts until validated with operators.

## Vertical launch method
1. Select one vertical and one workflow.
2. Conduct five to ten structured operator interviews.
3. Map the current workflow and measurable cost of failure.
4. Identify system of record and data permissions.
5. Create a synthetic-data demonstration.
6. Offer a bounded pilot with written acceptance criteria.
7. Review outcomes, cost, support burden, and repeatability.
8. Only then create a reusable vertical package.

## Sales system
Use problem-led discovery. Sell one workflow outcome with scope, exclusions, implementation time, support, data handling, and rollback. Track inbound interest and referrals; this plan sends no unsolicited outreach.

## Operating controls
No safety-critical or regulated decision is automated without qualified review. Human override remains available. Customer data is segregated. Integrations use least privilege. Changes are versioned. Pilots can be disabled without corrupting the customer's system of record.

## Metrics
Interview-to-pilot interest; time saved; exception resolution; data error rate; adoption; downtime; implementation hours; margin; verified outcomes.

## Release gates
Vertical selected from evidence; workflow owner defined; interviews recorded; synthetic demo validated; data/safety review; pilot acceptance and rollback approved.

## Status
Construction, logistics, and manufacturing are proposed market lanes. No industry-specific production deployment or customer outcome is asserted.
