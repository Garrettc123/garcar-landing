# Garcar Enterprise — Full Product Portfolio Requirements

Version: 1.0  
Status: Product requirements and build specifications; not a claim of completed or deployed products.

This document expands every catalog entry into a buildable product definition. Prices are hypotheses only. Infrastructure and governance items are internal capabilities unless explicitly sold as a service. Every customer-facing product requires validation, implementation, QA, security review, owner approval, and release evidence.

## Product definition standard
Each product must have: target user; user problem; primary outcome; MVP scope; explicit exclusions; inputs and outputs; acceptance tests; delivery/support model; measurable metrics; security/data rules; commercial validation gate.

## Division 2 — Revenue Automation

### RA-001 — Revenue Leak / Lead Response Audit
- Buyer: owner/operator or sales manager at a home-service business.
- Problem: inbound leads are missed, delayed, duplicated, or not measured.
- MVP: structured intake; review of owner-authorized sample records; response-time baseline; funnel map; prioritized remediation report.
- Outputs: findings, baseline, impact assumptions, recommended workflow, 30-day action list.
- Excludes: guaranteed revenue lift; unauthorized access; live messaging.
- Acceptance: sample coverage stated; baseline calculation reproducible; every recommendation linked to evidence.
- Delivery: fixed-scope audit with written acceptance.
- Proposed price hypothesis: $497.

### RA-002 — Speed-to-Lead Starter Installation
- Buyer: contractor with an existing lead source and named workflow owner.
- MVP: one lead source, deduplication, acknowledgement draft/approved message, staff assignment, status tracking, exception queue, audit log.
- Excludes: multi-CRM migrations, autonomous quoting, unapproved SMS/email, guaranteed bookings.
- Acceptance: synthetic test leads route correctly; duplicate and timeout cases handled; staff can override; rollback tested.
- Proposed price hypothesis: $2,500.

### RA-003 — Managed Lead Response
- Buyer: business with a proven starter workflow and support owner.
- MVP: monitoring, exception queue review, monthly response-time report, controlled workflow updates, support SLA.
- Excludes: unlimited custom development or guaranteed sales.
- Acceptance: service hours, severity definitions, response targets, exclusions, and monthly report agreed in contract.
- Proposed price hypothesis: $750–$1,500/month.

### RA-004 — Missed Call Recovery Pilot
- Buyer: contractor with call volume and a lawful callback/text process.
- MVP: call event capture, missed-call alert to staff, approved callback task, opt-out/consent checks where messaging is used, recovery tracking.
- Excludes: unsolicited messaging, recording calls without required notice/consent, autonomous promises to customers.
- Acceptance: synthetic calls tested; opt-out blocks messaging; manual fallback works; audit records retained.
- Price: discovery-based; no price until scope is known.

## Portfolio-wide definition of done
A product is not “complete” because its specification or UI exists. Done means: scope approved; implementation committed; automated tests pass; security/privacy checks pass; documented limitations; operator runbook; rollback/recovery; customer acceptance where applicable; owner authorizes release. Revenue is recognized only after live processor confirmation and reconciliation. No message is sent and no paid service is activated by this portfolio document.
