# Garcar Enterprise — Implementation Roadmap and Evidence Gates

## Phase 0 — Inventory and preserve
Freeze baseline; do not overwrite secrets, configuration, or payment flows. Inventory repositories, branches, services, product artifacts, test suites, deployments, and account limits. Classify each asset as verified, partial, planned, blocked, or unknown. Map assets to divisions and identify duplicates.

Exit evidence: repository inventory, artifact index, test status, deployment status, risk register. No paid resources or external messages.

## Phase 1 — Zero-cost demand validation
Prioritize Revenue Automation (one contractor lead-response problem), Digital Products (four existing guides), then AI Operations Studio (one narrow workflow). Validate other divisions through operator interviews and synthetic demos. Target five qualified conversations per offer/market hypothesis. Track objections and willingness to pay; do not manufacture testimonials or imply sales.

Exit evidence: interview log, validated problem, buyer role, pricing feedback, proceed/stop decision.

## Phase 2 — Productize and test
Convert validated offers into fixed scopes and acceptance criteria. Improve manuscripts/templates from feedback. Build synthetic demos. Add local fixtures and automated tests. Document security, privacy, support, rollback.

Exit evidence: versioned deliverable, test results, known limitations, customer acceptance criteria.

## Phase 3 — Sandbox commercial flow (explicit owner authorization required)
Create separate digital-product and service catalog IDs. Configure test-mode checkout only after approval. Verify signatures, idempotency, product/price match, paid-only entitlement, refunds, access expiry. Test consent/unsubscribe logic without sending to real contacts.

Exit evidence: reproducible test report and owner review. Test transactions are not revenue.

## Phase 4 — Production pilot (separate explicit authorization required)
Confirm contract and data permissions; infrastructure budget and service health; threat model and rollback rehearsal; exact deployment, messaging, and payment approvals. Verify health and reconcile collections.

Exit evidence: approval record, deployment identifier, smoke tests, monitoring, incident path, payment reconciliation.

## Phase 5 — Scale only what works
Expand one validated product or vertical at a time using contribution margin, support load, retention, reliability, and customer evidence.

## Portfolio priorities
| Priority | Division | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| P0 | Governance & Security | Shared safety and evidence foundation |
| P1 | Revenue Automation | Clear B2B pain and service-led first-cash path |
| P1 | Digital Products | Existing assets enable low-cost validation |
| P2 | AI Operations Studio | Reusable delivery after a narrow pilot |
| P3 | Industry Solutions | Verticalize validated workflows |
| P3 | Wealth Forge | Validate demand and calculation quality |
| P4 | Enterprise Intelligence | Higher proof, security, and enterprise sales burden |
| Continuous | Infrastructure | Support validated work; avoid premature spend |

## Stop conditions
Stop or narrow scope if no buyer problem is confirmed, costs exceed approved ceiling, permissions are missing, tests fail, data access is unsafe, or delivery is unreliable.
