# Division 02 — Garcar Revenue Automation

## Mission and customer
Help home-service businesses respond to leads faster, recover missed opportunities, and measure conversion. Initial target segment: Texas HVAC, plumbing, roofing, restoration, and electrical contractors. Small firms with an observable inbound-lead problem are a targeting hypothesis.

## Product catalog
| Offer | Offer | Proposed price hypothesis | Deliverable |
|---|---|---:|---|
| RA-001 | Revenue Leak / Lead Response Audit | $497 | Findings, response-time baseline, prioritized fixes |
| RA-002 | Speed-to-Lead Starter Installation | $2,500 | One agreed lead intake and response workflow |
| RA-003 | Managed Lead Response | $750–$1,500/month | Monitoring, exception handling, monthly report |
| RA-004 | Missed Call Recovery Pilot | Scope after discovery | Controlled callback/text workflow; consent review required |

These are proposed prices, not active quotes, commitments, or successful sales.

## Sales system
- Buyer: owner/operator or sales manager.
- Trigger: missed calls, delayed form response, duplicate leads, poor appointment conversion, or absent response-time reporting.
- Discovery: lead sources, weekly volume, current process, baseline response time, booking rate, system constraints.
- Proof: read-only audit or synthetic demo; no customer production access without written permission.
- Proposal: fixed scope, exclusions, acceptance criteria, data handling, support window, price, rollback.
- Stages: target → researched → permission checked → discovery → qualified → proposal → contract → paid/active → delivered → renewal.
- No cold email or automated SMS is sent by this plan. Future outreach requires explicit approval and lawful contact basis.

## Delivery system
1. Intake and written scope.
2. Baseline measurement using customer-approved data.
3. Workflow and failure-point map.
4. Sandbox build with synthetic records.
5. Customer acceptance test.
6. Human-approved production cutover.
7. Monitoring, exception handling, evidence report.

## Controls
No guaranteed revenue lift or booked jobs. No automated customer messaging until authorization, consent, opt-out handling, and applicable rules are reviewed. Use least privilege; preserve secrets; record workflow version, tests, approval, rollback, and outcome.

## Metrics
Time to first response; lead-to-contact; contact-to-booking; missed-call recovery; duplicate rate; workflow failure rate; customer-reported outcome; implementation margin; verified collections.

## Release gates
One narrow use case; five qualified discovery conversations; baseline and value hypothesis; synthetic demo pass; written data permission; support and rollback plan; verified payment before work is classified as paid.

## Status
Historical context includes Garcar Revenue OS, Apex Revenue System, Deal Desk, and lead-response concepts. Their integration and production reliability are not established by this specification.
